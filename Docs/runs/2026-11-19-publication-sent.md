# Change notes of 1.1.0 and 1.0.0, moved from PUBLICATION.md on 2026-11-19 (already sent)

### 1.1.0

Steam change note of 1.1.0 (published 2026-11-16); its first line carries the version. The full list is `CHANGELOG.md`,
section `## [1.1.0]`. 1.0.0 went out on 2026-10-13 with `[b]1.0.0[/b]`.

```
[b]1.1.0[/b]
[list]
[*] The mingshe's wind barrier no longer repeats the same error on every tick.
[*] English and French wording corrected after a full translation review; the French was proofread by a native speaker.
[*] The description now says which language was completed with AI: Simplified Chinese, extended with Claude and reviewed by ChatGPT, not checked by a native speaker.
[*] New gallery and preview.
[*] Smaller download: two oversized original textures removed.
[/list]

### 1.0.0 (note rewritten 2026-11-16)

Posted by Virginie on the Steam change-notes page on 2026-11-16, replacing the pointer sentence of 2026-10-13.

```
[b]1.0.0[/b]
[list]
[*] The beasts now rest and breed. Tame and hostile beasts need rest like any animal; a tame pair mates and has a baby that grows up (baby, juvenile, adult). The mingshe and the Pleiades star officer lay eggs instead. Clones saved before this version stay genderless and cannot breed.
[*] Optional: Nocturnal Animals. With it, the qiongqi and the sexie hunt by night and the other beasts keep dawn and dusk hours; without it they follow the normal clock.
[*] Optional: A Dog Said... Animal Prosthetics 2. The five tame clones accept its prosthetics and bionics like a wolf or a thrumbo, and the Pleiades star officer like a duck. The mod now loads before it.
[*] The qiongqi's claw gene is no longer named after the nian beast ("qiongqi's blessing" and "qiongqi's gift").
[*] French and English wording touched up: the crow's song, the sexie's aura ("deadly rage"), the mingshe's sound wave.
[*] The nian beast's description no longer promises nian beast fangs: butchering it gives meat only.
[*] Fixed: the mingshe's wind barrier no longer throws an error when it destroys a projectile twice.
[*] Fixed: the development action "Beast attack now" now really forces its incident.
[*] New Preview image.
[/list]
